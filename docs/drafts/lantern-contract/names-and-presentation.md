# Names and presentation

> **DRAFT — DO NOT USE.**
> This is a draft. It should not be used in any way, shape, or form for now. Do not put these names on a title screen, a card, a listing, or a commission. Do not treat this file as legal advice or as a cleared title.

## Working title

**The Count's Hall.**

The dealer marker is the Chair. A round is a watch. A player's written number is a contract. Tricks stay called tricks. This title has not been searched. It is a label for the draft.

The packet files still sit in `docs/drafts/lantern-contract/`.

The ladder is one sentence: a Fool beats the Count, the Count beats the Knights, a Knight beats a Fool. Steel beats Grain, Stone, and Wool. Cloaks lose.

## The people

- **The Count** commands the hall. One card. Loses only to a Fool.
- **The Fools** mock the Count. Two cards. They lose to Knights.
- **The Squire** is not a Knight until you say so. One card. Say armed or cloaked as it is played.
- **Marshal** names who leads next.
- **Steward** reads the top of the undealt pile and puts it back in a chosen order.
- **Exchanger** takes the top card and puts one card, which may be that same card, out of the game face down.
- **Almoner** can hand the trick they just won to a player who still needs tricks for their bid.

The four named fighters are Knights. They share a rank and differ only in the job.

No one in this set is a skeleton, a pirate, or a fighter holding a surrender flag. The Fools are jesters. They are not figures in the water.

## Suits

Four suits, ranks 1 through 14. The 14 of each suit is a Tithe.

| Suit | Job | Suggested ink, if art is ever made |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grain | Plain | Wheat gold |
| Stone | Plain | Warm grey |
| Wool | Plain | Russet |
| Steel | Trump | Iron black |

A plain Tithe pays 10. The Steel Tithe pays 20. Steel is metal, not a flag, and it does not use a skull.

## Modes and formats

| Name | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Stake | Sharp scoring. A made bid pays 15 per trick bid. A made zero pays 8 per card. Bonuses pay only if the bid is true. |
| Glide | Even scoring. Every exact bid is the same pot. Off by one is half. Off by two or more is nothing. |
| Full watch | Hands 1 through 10 |
| Half watch | Hands 3, 4, 5, 6, 5, 4 |
| Hall rounds | Hands 4, 4, 5, 5, 6, 6, 7 |

## Words that stay out of this draft

Skull King. Tigress. Mermaid. Pirate. Escape. Kraken. White Whale. Loot. Rascal. Jolly Roger. The published names of the original's alternate hand sizes. Do not use those words as card names, mode names, rank names, or as a subtitle that tells a stranger which game this resembles.

## If art is ever briefed

Do not brief anyone from this draft now. When a later, cleared card list exists, the limits to carry forward are:

- The Count is a living noble in a wool coat. No crown of bones, no skeleton, no crossed swords as the silhouette.
- The Squire's two choices are a sword at the hip and a hooded cloak. The cloak is not a white flag of surrender.
- The Fools are jesters in motley. Not figures with tails.
- The Knights should be readable at card size by silhouette and by name: a pointing marshal, a steward with a stack of stores, an exchanger with two coins, an almoner passing a purse.
- Cloaks are four hooded cloaks and should look like a set.
- The number cards are ranks 1 through 14 in Grain, Stone, Wool, and Steel. The 14 of each suit is marked as a Tithe.
- Truce, Smoke, and the Oath should look like a snapped staff, a field lost in smoke, and a sealed bond. They should not look like a sea-monster pair plus a treasure card.
- Card banners use the names in this file and no other product's title.

## What is not in this draft

No ladder of player ranks. No wager on the bid. No second Oath. No fifth Knight. No blank cards left for the table to invent a person. No two-player ghost hand. Those are absences on purpose, so the first version stays learnable in one watch.
