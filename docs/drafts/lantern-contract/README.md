## What this draft is for

A night of The Count's Hall should feel like the night you already know. You still look at a hand, write a number, reveal together, follow suit, save specials for the moment they matter, and live with the number you wrote. The numbered cards run from 1 to 14, one suit trumps, and the 14s are the bonus ranks. Someone who has played the original should finish learning during the first watch, not during the first hour.

## Vocabulary this draft uses

The Count's hall. The Chair. A watch. A contract. Stake and Glide. Grain, Stone, Wool, and Steel. Tithes (the 14s). Cloak. The Count. The Fools. The Squire. Marshal, Steward, Exchanger, and Almoner. Truce, Smoke, and the Oath. True, Near, and Wide.

The ladder, said once: a Fool beats the Count, the Count beats the Knights, a Knight beats a Fool. Steel beats Grain, Stone, and Wool. Cloaks lose.
