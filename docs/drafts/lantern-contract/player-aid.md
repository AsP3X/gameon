## Turn

Deal clockwise from the dealer's left. Secret bids from 0 to the hand size. Reveal together. The player left of the dealer leads. Winner leads next. Pass the Chair clockwise after the watch.

## Legal plays

Follow the led suit if you have it. A special may always be played. Another suit's number may not, and Steel is a suit.

The led suit is the first card that is not a Cloak, and only if that card has a number. The Oath and the Squire said as cloaked are Cloaks. A person, Truce, or Smoke sets no suit.

The Squire is not played until you say **armed** or **cloaked**.

## Ladder

Stop at the first line that fits.

1. Truce and Smoke together: both are Cloaks. Keep going.
2. Truce alone: nobody wins. Next lead is clockwise from the Truce player.
3. Smoke alone: trump is off, specials cannot win. Highest of the led suit wins. With no led suit, highest rank wins and the earlier card breaks a tie. No number card: nobody wins, Smoke's player leads.
4. A Fool and the Count: the earliest Fool wins.
5. The Count: the Count wins.
6. A Knight, including the Squire armed: the earliest Knight wins.
7. A Fool: the earliest Fool wins.
8. Numbers, in play order: the first number is ahead. A later card moves ahead if it is a higher card of that same suit, or if it is Steel and the card ahead is not. A higher Steel moves ahead of a lower Steel. A different plain suit never moves ahead.
9. All Cloaks: the earliest card wins.

Cloaks lose to numbers. Numbers lose to a Fool. A Fool loses to a Knight. A Knight loses to the Count. The Count loses to a Fool.

Ranks are 1 through 14. A 14 is a Tithe: 10 if it is Grain, Stone, or Wool, and 20 if it is Steel.

## Powers

Only the Knight who won, and only if you want it. The Squire has no power.

| Card | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Marshal | Name the next leader. Skip on the last trick. |
| Steward | Secretly reorder the top three of the pile. Skip on the last trick. |
| Exchanger | Draw one, discard one face down, out of the game. Needs a card left in hand and a card in the pile. |
| Almoner | Give the trick to a player who is still short of their bid, or keep it. A clean zero is not short. |

## Bonus raw

Add one total, then scale that total. Do not scale line by line.

| Event | Raw |
| --- | ---: |
| Plain 14 | 10 |
| Steel 14 | 20 |
| Knight wins, per Fool | 10 |
| Count wins, per Knight | 15 |
| Fool wins against the Count | 25 |
| Each side of an Oath | 10 |

Fool against the Count, with a Knight also in the trick: **25 only**.

| Accuracy | Stake bonuses | Glide bonuses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| True, exact bid | All of it | All of it |
| Near, off by 1 | 0 | Half, fraction dropped |
| Wide, off by 2 or more | 0 | 0 |

If either Oath partner is Wide, leave the Oath's 10 out of both totals before that scale.

## Stake

| Result | Score |
| --- | --- |
| True, bid 1+ | `15 × bid` |
| True, bid 0 | `8 × cards` |
| Miss, bid 1+ | `−6 × the difference` |
| Miss, bid 0 | `−8 × cards` |

Then add bonuses only on a true bid.

A made bid of 5 is 75. A made bid of 6 is 90. A made zero on a 10-card watch is 80.

## Glide

Pot is `8 × cards` for every bid, including zero. True takes the pot. Near takes half. Wide takes 0. Add the scaled bonuses. The watch score is never negative.
